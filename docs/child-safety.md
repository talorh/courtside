# Child safety: the site against the Child Safe Standards

State basketball bodies in Australia ask clubs and associations to meet the Child Safe Standards (Victoria's eleven are
the example here; `src/club.ts` names the club's own body and links its standards). Most of them are about the club's people,
culture and processes. This page checks what the **site** does for each one, and says what stays with the club.

Reviewed, again for photos, and again for several teams. Review it each
season, when the season's date changes, and with each new feature. Help is for using the site, so its child safety
question, Standards, doesn't restate them or tell people what to do: it says the site makes its best efforts to meet
the standards body's Child Safe Standards (linked), that only game footage is filmed, and that the club's
handbook and/or website cover child safety policies. The site isn't the club's own, so it doesn't speak for the club.

| # | Standard | What the site does | Stays with the club and the team |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Culturally safe for Aboriginal children | Nothing specific to a video site; no content is public | Club culture and policy |
| 2 | Safety embedded in leadership and culture | Each team's team manager runs their team, decides who gets in and handles removal requests. The site's admins look after the site as a whole and can open any team, to help | Club child safety policy |
| 3 | Children empowered, taken seriously | Any family can ask for a video, clip or photo to be removed, and can ask that their child isn't filmed, photographed or shown (Help) | Listening to the children themselves |
| 4 | Families informed and involved | Help explains what is filmed and photographed, who can see it, how long it's kept and how to ask for removal. B12.1's families were told before launch; each new team's team manager does the same, and collects consent on the club's form | |
| 5 | Equity and diverse needs | Plain English, works in any phone's browser, nothing to install | |
| 6 | Suitable, supported people | Only the team manager adds people. Uploading and Go live only for those set to Watch and film, the coaches and the team manager; adding photos for them too; only the team manager deletes | Working with Children Checks for coaches and officials |
| 7 | Child-focused complaints process | Removal requests from any video, clip or photo go to the team manager, and Help points to the Saints handbook and/or website for child safety policies | The club's complaints process |
| 8 | Knowledge and training | Help links the standards body's Child Safe Standards rather than restating them | Club training |
| 9 | Safe physical and online environments | Private behind the site's own sign-in; each team sees only its own; nothing public, `noindex`, search engines and AI bots blocked; players by name and number, only inside the site; videos and photos deleted at the end of the season's year, originals after 30 days; the download log kept 2 years; no filming where a competition doesn't allow it (No video); photos made smaller on the phone with their location left off, stripped again by the site, and shown only through links that run out | What people do with a downloaded file |
| 10 | Regularly reviewed and improved | This page, reviewed each season and with each feature | Club review |
| 11 | Policies and procedures document safety | `docs/spec.md` (Privacy, safety and security), this page, and Help | Club and the standards body policies |

Gaps the site can't close: a downloaded video or photo can be shared (Help points to the standards body's standards
rather than telling people what not to do), and other parents may film or take photos on their own
phones. Both are club and family matters.
