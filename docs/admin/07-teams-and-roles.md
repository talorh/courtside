# 7. Teams and roles

*Courtside Documentation › How it works. [Contents](README.md).*

Each team is one season's squad. A person can be in several teams, with a role in each. Roles come only from the team's list in D1.

| Role | Can do |
| --- | --- |
| Watch only | Watch, download, make highlight clips, star favourites, add photos and delete their own, and ask for something to be removed |
| Watch and film | Everything Watch only can, plus upload a game's video and Go live (the usual role for a parent) |
| Coach | Everything Watch and film can, plus private notes on each game |
| Team manager | Runs the team: people, players, the fixture, the roster, scores, deleting things and removal requests |
| Site admin | This admin area, and any team, opened as its team manager or viewed as any role |

A new team's Manage team starts with a setup checklist (at least 7 players in the squad; someone set to Watch and film, the coach and a team manager; the fixture; the roster), gone once it's all done; admin Teams shows each running team's progress on it.

> **A team's life on the site.** A team is onboarded, runs its season (its setup checklist first), is closed at the season's end and becomes read-only, and is deleted with its footage on 31 December of the year its season ends or on a night an admin picks. A closed team can be reopened until deleting starts. Deleting takes every video, clip, photo, original and record of the team, except what its download log names, which is kept its two years.

A team is running, then closed at the end of its season (read-only straight away), then deleted with all its footage on the night it's due: 31 December of the year its season ends, unless an admin picks an earlier night. Until then, Manage team's end-of-season pack lists every game's HD video (with one button to get them all made), each player's clips and where to save the photos and roster.
